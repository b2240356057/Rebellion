The game is about a geurilla camp post-apocalypse. 
Every round an event or a folk appears and speaks about the problem that the camp has. 
Sometimes folk is angry sometimes folk is sad sometimes folk is neutral just clarifying his desires.
You are given 2 choices every round that choice determines your camps destiny.
You are trying to balance 4 stats till the end of the game.
There are 2 ends: 
1. WinState: Population reaches 100 and camp is big enough to be an army to conquer world.           
2. LoseState: Motivation or supply drops under 0 and folk goes rebell and kill you or directly rebellion bar fills and folk rebells.
